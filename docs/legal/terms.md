viel dir die Unterstützung wert ist, nicht, was du bekommst. Die „Testpressung" ist eine
einmalige Zahlung; sie schaltet dasselbe frei, solange wir den gehosteten Dienst betreiben

Den Schlüssel trägst du in der App ein. Er steht für deine Stufe, nicht für dein
Discogs-Konto.

### Terms for the Sleeveside supporter tier

As of: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

There are two contracts here, with different partners.

- **You buy from {{MOR_LEGAL_NAME}}** ("Lemon Squeezy"). Lemon Squeezy is the seller and
  merchant of record: it takes your payment, issues the invoice, pays the VAT, renews your

- **The service promise comes from {{NAME}}** ("we"), who runs Sleeveside (address in the

Sleeveside is not affiliated with Discogs. Discogs is party to neither contract.

#### 2. The app stays free

The Sleeveside app is and stays free and fully usable without support: every signal, every
screen, self-hosting included. The shop watcher and the audio preview are never part of a
paid tier. No paid feature calls the Discogs API on your behalf.

#### 3. What you get

Support gets you an **access key**. While it is valid, it unlocks on the servers we run:

- **"Everywhere"**: hosted, end-to-end-encrypted sync between your devices, unlocked with a
  passkey. We store the data sealed and cannot read it.
- **"Instant catalogue"**: lookups in our catalogue, built from Discogs' public CC0 data.

All yearly tiers (7", LP, double album, first pressing) unlock the same; you choose what the
support is worth to you, not what you get. The "test pressing" is a one-off payment; it
unlocks the same for as long as we run the hosted service (section 8).

You enter the key in the app. It stands for your tier, not for your Discogs account.

#### 4. Availability

We run the servers on a best-effort basis. We promise no particular availability and give
no service-level agreement. Maintenance, host outages and failures happen; we announce
planned maintenance where we can. If the hosted service is down, the app keeps working on
your device.

Your statutory rights for defects in digital products (§§ 327 ff. BGB) are unaffected.

#### 5. Changes to the service

We may change the service for a valid reason — a change in the law, security, or a change at

usefulness more than trivially, we tell you in good time, and you may end the contract free
of charge within 30 days under § 327r BGB.

#### 6. Term, renewal, cancellation

- Yearly tiers run for one year and renew until you cancel. Lemon Squeezy handles renewal,
  charging and cancellation.
- You can cancel at any time, effective at the end of the current period — through the
  "Cancel contracts here" link in the app or directly in Lemon Squeezy's customer portal

- After the end your key stops renewing and expires within 35 days at most. The app keeps
  working without it.
- We may terminate for good cause or revoke a key, for example on a chargeback or abuse
  (such as handing the key to others).

#### 7. Withdrawal

Your right of withdrawal from the purchase is against Lemon Squeezy; you get the withdrawal
notice there before you buy. The app has a "Withdraw from contract" link that leads there.

#### 8. If we stop running the hosted service

If we stop, we say so at least three months ahead. Yearly tiers then stop renewing.

#### 11. Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the new version applies only from your next renewal. We send it to
you at least six weeks before. If you do not agree, cancel by the end of the current period;
nothing changes for you until then. Your silence does not count as consent to changes in the

German law applies, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of
Goods. If you are a consumer habitually resident in another country, you keep the
protection of that country's mandatory rules (Art. 6(2) Rome I Regulation).

We do not take part in dispute resolution proceedings before a consumer arbitration board
and are not obliged to (§ 36 VSBG).

one (§ 306 BGB).
